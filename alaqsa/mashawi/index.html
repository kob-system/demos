    Cook it right

    Use a meat thermometer. Wash your hands, board and tongs after touching raw meat.

    165°F

    Chicken, including chicken kofta.

    160°F

    Ground beef or lamb kofta.

    145°F

    Beef or lamb cubes, then rest 3 minutes.

    Temperatures follow the USDA safe minimums.
