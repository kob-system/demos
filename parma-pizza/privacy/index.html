What this site collects

This website has no forms, no accounts and no tracking pixels. It does not set cookies and does not collect your name, phone number or email.

Tools this page loads

  • Google Fonts serves the lettering. Google receives your IP address when the font loads.
  • An embedded Google Maps map on the home page. Google's privacy policy applies to it.
  • Links to our online ordering page (run by SkyTab) and Instagram open those services, and their own privacy policies apply there.

Online orders

When you order online you are on the SkyTab ordering page, not this site. SkyTab handles your order, payment and contact details under its own privacy policy.
