Catering questions

How much food do I need?

A half tray feeds about 6 to 8. A 24-cut party pie gives 24 squares or slices. Tell us the headcount on the phone and we'll size it.

How far ahead should I order?

For trays and party pies, call the day before if you can. Same-day works for smaller orders.

Do you have gluten-free?

Yes, a 12 inch cauliflower crust pizza.
