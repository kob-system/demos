Questions

Good to know.

Are you in Halfmoon or Clifton Park?

Both, kind of. We're at 1525 Route 9, right on the Halfmoon / Clifton Park line. The mailing address says Clifton Park 12065.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 10 AM to 8:30 PM, Friday and Saturday 10 AM to 9 PM, Sunday noon to 8 PM.

Do you have gluten-free pizza?

Yes, a 12 inch cauliflower crust for $13.99.

Do you cater?

Yes. Half trays serve 6 to 8 and run $40 to $48, plus party-size Sicilian and thin pies. Call (518) 280-3288 to set it up.

Is it cheaper to order direct?

Yes. Delivery apps charge more per item. Order on our site or call us and you pay the shop price.

What's the best deal for a family?

The Double Play: 2 XL pizzas, 20 wings and a 2-liter for $59.99. Tuesdays and Wednesdays, any XL pickup comes with a free 2-liter.
