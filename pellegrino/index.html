Good to know

Which Pellegrino's is this?

M. Pellegrino Importing Co. at 1197 Central Ave, Albany. There are related Pellegrino stores in North Greenbush and Malta, each run on its own.

Can I call my order in ahead?

Yes. Call (518) 459-4472 and it will be wrapped and waiting.

Do you cater?

Yes. Use the catering request or call, and we build the trays with you.

Are you open Sundays?

No. Monday to Friday 9 to 5, Saturday 8 to 3, closed Sunday. Holiday hours are posted on Google.

Do you carry Greek products?

Yes. Italian and Greek specialty foods, side by side, like the sign says.
