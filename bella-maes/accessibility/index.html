Accessibility

DRAFT. Last updated October 2026.

We want everyone to be able to see our menu, find us and order. This site is built to work with screen readers, keyboard navigation and phone zoom, with readable contrast and labeled buttons. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.

Something not working?

If any part of this site is hard to use, call us at (518) 451-9474 and we will take your order or answer your question over the phone, and fix the page.

Online ordering

Online ordering runs on Slice, a separate service. If it gives you trouble, call us and we will take the order directly.
